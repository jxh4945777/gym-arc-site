Why Gym-Arc

Others tell you whether a rule was broken. We tell you what every step was worth

The same rule costs different amounts at different decision points. Gym-Arc computes that consequence structure, and that is what it takes to train a model that reads rules.

Every step has a price

At every decision point, every legal action is rolled out counterfactually to get its expected value and regret. Models learn trade-offs, beyond right and wrong.

No peeking at the answer

Labels use only what the model can observe. Switching to these labels cut decision loss in bancassurance sales by more than half.

Compliance is the floor

Fail any constraint and the episode scores 0. Business outcomes are reported apart and never enter the reward, so violations never pay.

Add a company, not code

Companies differ by one rules file. Adding one takes zero code and under a second from rules to a ready environment.

New industry, just declare it

Describe customers, actions, rules and rewards in one YAML file; the framework supplies simulator, gold, Verifier and experiments. Three new industries took 0 lines of domain code.

Results that survive an audit

Study designs are committed before training with git timestamps to prove it, and every number in the docs and console is re-checked by automated guards.

FAQ

Questions buyers ask

Do we have to share real customer data?

You don't start with customer data. The environment is built from your rulebook and tables; case history, when available, calibrates rules that only show up in outcomes, such as approval turnaround or underwriting strictness.

Who owns the data and the outputs?

The contract spells out five items: ownership of source data, the general framework, task and evaluation methods, synthetic assets derived from your rules, and whether anything may be licensed to other clients. It is in the agreement from day one.

Which models are supported?

Any model you can train through Gymnasium or Hugging Face TRL, plus custom structured decision models. General LLMs can be plugged in as baselines or judges.

Why not just give an LLM the rulebook?

An LLM that reads the rulebook still cannot tell what a rule is worth at this step. In our comparison, a decision model trained in the environment was 17.9 points more accurate than a general LLM given the rules, and 112× faster.

Can it run on our own servers?

Yes. Environments, onboarding and training are Python and run on your GPU servers; the console is a static page that also works offline.

How long does it take to onboard a company?

On the platform side there is no code to change, and rules become a ready environment in under a second. The overall timeline mostly depends on preparing documents; we provide a handbook skeleton, table templates and an intake questionnaire to get it right the first time.
